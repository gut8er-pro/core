# 02 — Remove the DAT/Integrations step from the signup wizard

**Category:** enhancement
**Status:** ready-for-agent

## Agent Brief

**Summary:** Remove the DAT/Integrations step from the signup wizard UX (keeping its code for future reuse), and make the Plan step the final step that creates the account.

**Current behavior:**
The signup wizard presents 5 steps ending in an Integrations step, where the user optionally connects a DAT calculation provider. That final step is **also where account creation happens**: the `completeSignup` server action — which creates the Supabase auth user, the Prisma `User`/`Business` records, and the Stripe Checkout session — is invoked only from the Integrations step (via its "Create an Account" and "Skip" actions). The wizard chrome (the ordered step list, the step-key→number map, the progress-bar percentage, and the stepper sidebar) is built around 5 steps. `completeSignup` already tolerates empty integrations (it only creates an integration record when a valid provider is present).

**Desired behavior:**
- The Integrations step is removed from the user-facing flow, leaving 4 steps: Account → Personal → Business → Plan. It is not shown in the stepper and is not reachable by normal forward navigation.
- The **Plan step becomes the final step**. Its primary CTA becomes "Create an Account" and triggers the same `completeSignup` call with empty integrations — including the Stripe Checkout redirect on success, the same inline error handling on failure, the signup-store reset, and a pending/disabled state while submitting.
- The Integrations step code is **preserved, not deleted** — commented out or otherwise dormant, with a note that it is intentionally parked for a future re-introduction. This covers the Integrations step component, its `/signup/integrations` route, the `integrations` slice + setter in the signup store, and the `signupIntegrationsSchema` validation.
- No backend change is required: `completeSignup` already no-ops on empty integrations.

**Key interfaces:**
- **Wizard step configuration:** the ordered step list and the step-key→number map must no longer present Integrations as a reachable step; the terminal "complete" state shifts accordingly. The progress calculation and stepper sidebar must reflect 4 steps rather than 5 (progress reaches 100% at the Plan step).
- **Plan step:** its terminal action moves from "navigate forward to Integrations" to invoking `completeSignup` with `integrations: {}`, mirroring the Integrations step's account-creation behavior (Stripe `checkoutUrl` redirect on success, inline error on failure, store `reset()`, submitting state).
- **Single source of truth:** relocate the account-creation logic (the `completeSignup` invocation plus its error/loading/checkout-redirect/reset handling) out of the Integrations step and into the Plan step — do not duplicate it in both places.
- **Preserved-but-dormant:** the Integrations step component, its route, the `integrations` store slice, and `signupIntegrationsSchema` remain in the repo, commented/unreferenced, clearly marked as parked for future use.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The signup wizard shows exactly 4 steps; Integrations does not appear in the stepper and is not reachable by normal forward navigation.
- [ ] The progress bar reaches 100% at the Plan step, and step numbering/labels are consistent for 4 steps.
- [ ] The Plan step's primary CTA creates the account by calling `completeSignup` with empty integrations, and redirects to Stripe Checkout on success, with the same error surface the Integrations step previously had.
- [ ] Completing signup from the Plan step produces the same `User`/`Business` records and Stripe Checkout behavior as before; no integration record is created (handled by the existing `completeSignup` guard).
- [ ] The Integrations step code (component, `/signup/integrations` route, store slice, schema) still exists in the repo, commented out or otherwise dormant, with a note that it is parked for future use.
- [ ] Back-navigation across the 4 steps and the "complete" success page both work end-to-end.

**Out of scope:**
- Deleting Integrations-related code, DB columns/relations, or the integrations handling inside `completeSignup`.
- Adding Audatex / GT Motive providers.
- Changing what the Plan step collects (still Pro plan only) or altering the Stripe Checkout logic itself.
- Integrations configuration in Settings — it already exists there and needs no change.
